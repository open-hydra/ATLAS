# Databases

ATLAS bundles a curated set of thermodynamic, transport, and chemistry databases covering a wide range of species and conditions relevant to propulsion and high-enthalpy flows.

---

## :material-thermometer: Thermodynamics

---
**Models & Data Sources**

- NASA 9-coefficient polynomials (NASA Glenn)
- Burcat extended thermodynamic database  
- Real-fluid equations of state (Peng–Robinson, Redlich–Kwong)
- CoolProp database for real fluids
- Custom thermodynamic database

[→ Open Thermodynamics](thermodynamics/index.md)

---

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Transport

---
**Methods & Correlations**

- CEA curve-fit transport polynomials

- Custom transport database
